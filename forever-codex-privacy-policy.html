Privacy Policy – Forever Codex

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Forever Codex is an unofficial, fan-made companion app for World of Warcraft: Forever. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Blizzard Entertainment.

What we collect

We do not run any servers for the app and we do not collect, store or receive your personal data. There is no account or sign-in. Your saved builds, shopping lists and favourites are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and similar data to show and measure ads. In the EU, UK and other regions where required, the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time with the "Ad privacy choices" button on the About screen. On iOS you can also manage tracking in system settings. See Google's advertising policy and privacy policy. Codex+ subscribers see no ads.

Purchases

Codex+ is bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by the store; we do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
