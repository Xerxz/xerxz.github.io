Kingdom Planner ("the app") is an unofficial planner for players of Kingshot, developed by Xerxz. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data stored on your device

The speed-ups, bonuses, Town Center and training plans, rally leaders and event reminders you enter are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us, and the app has no account or sign-in.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. Your phone's backup system may include it if you have device backup turned on.

Notifications

Event reminders are scheduled locally on your device. If you add a reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications. No reminder data leaves your device.

Advertising

The free version shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Google Play (or the Apple App Store on iPhone). We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation of what you have purchased so it can unlock Premium.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Not affiliated

Kingdom Planner is a fan-made tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Century Games or Kingshot.
