Privacy policy · Effective August 22, 2026

Privacy in plain language

PalMap works without an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

No trackingNo advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.
No analyticsNo analytics, telemetry, or advertising SDKs.
Local progressVisited locations remain on your device.

Information stored on your device

PalMap stores visited-location identifiers locally so it can remember your progress. This information is not transmitted to the developer or third parties. Deleting the app removes this locally stored information.

Purchases

PalMap Pro purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. PalMap checks Apple-signed transaction entitlements to unlock Pro features. The developer does not receive or store payment-card details.

Children’s privacy

PalMap does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users because the app does not collect personal data.

Policy changes

If PalMap’s privacy practices change, this page and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the relevant version is released.
