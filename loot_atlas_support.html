Common questions

Is this an official app?

No. Loot Atlas is an unofficial companion made by a fan. See the disclaimer at the bottom of this page.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Maps, search, loot, bosses, builds, progress and the farm tracker all work without a connection once content is installed.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. There are no accounts and no cloud saves, so deleting the app deletes your progress permanently. On iPhone and iPad, offloading the app keeps your data; deleting it does not.

How do I move my progress to a new device?

Go to More › Data backup & export, tap Export progress file and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new device. On the new device, open the same screen and tap Import from file. Export and import are part of Loot Atlas Pro.

How do I restore Loot Atlas Pro?

Open More › Loot Atlas Pro and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by the stores, so refunds are too. Request one from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com or from Google through Google Play's refund page.

The drop rate looks wrong

The rate in the farm tracker is your own sample: the drops you logged divided by the kills you logged. It isn't the game's real drop rate and becomes more reliable the more you farm.

Some game data is wrong or missing

Game data is maintained by the community. Each drop source is labelled Verified, Community reported or Unknown. If something is wrong, email us the item, boss or location name and what you saw in the game.

How do I hide spoilers?

Go to More › Appearance, map & spoilers and choose a level under Spoiler protection. Hidden entries stay masked until you reveal them or change the setting.

How do I start over?

Open More › Profiles and tap Reset profile progress. This can't be undone, so export a copy first if you might want it back.

Why does my screen stay on?

During a farm session the app keeps the screen awake so you can count kills without unlocking your phone. It goes back to normal when the session ends.
