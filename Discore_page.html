Privacy Policy

Effective: 20 August 2026  ·  App: Discore for Android and Wear OS

The short version: Discore has no account system, ads or developer-operated analytics. Scores and player names are stored locally. Approximate location is optional and is only used to find nearby courses. Discore does not sell personal data.

1. Who is responsible

This policy applies to the Discore mobile and Wear OS apps and this promotional website. The app publisher and data controller is Xerxz (“Discore”, “we”, “us”). Contact details are provided at the end of this policy.

2. Data stored on your device

Discore stores the information needed to run the app locally on your Android phone:

  • player names you enter;
  • course names, hole definitions, pars, distances and scores;
  • active and completed rounds;
  • app settings such as theme, default players and screen-awake preference;
  • cached nearby-course results, course coordinates and the approximate origin of the latest course search.

We do not operate an account or cloud database for this information and do not receive it on a Discore server. Android may include app data in device or cloud backups depending on your Android and Google backup settings.

3. Optional approximate location

Discore requests Android’s approximate location permission so it can sort and find disc golf courses near you. The permission is optional; manual scorecards work without it. Discore does not request background location or precise GPS permission.

When nearby-course search runs, latitude and longitude are included in a search sent to a public Overpass API endpoint. The endpoint can therefore receive the query coordinates, your IP address, app identifier, device/network metadata and request time in its normal server logs. No player names or scorecards are included in that request.

The latest search origin is saved locally to decide whether cached course data should be refreshed. It remains on the device until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled, subject to Android backup behavior.

4. Wear OS synchronization

If you use the Wear OS app, the active round—including course, players, holes and scores—is synchronized between your paired phone and watch through Google Play Services’ Wearable Data Layer. This is required to score from the watch. Discore does not send this information to a developer-operated server.

6. What Discore does not include

The current version of Discore contains no advertising SDK, no developer-operated analytics or telemetry SDK, no social login and no in-app account system. We do not sell, rent or use personal data for targeted advertising.

7. Retention and security

Local round history and settings remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. Cached data may be replaced as course searches are refreshed. Data is protected by Android’s application sandbox and your device security. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and deletion controls

  • Deny or revoke approximate location in Android Settings; manual scoring remains available.
  • Clear completed round history from Discore → Settings → Data → Clear history.
  • Clear all local Discore data through Android’s app settings or uninstall the app.
  • Manage Android backup and restore behavior in your device’s Google backup settings.
  • Disconnect or uninstall the Wear OS app to stop watch synchronization.

Because we do not keep a Discore account or developer-operated user database, we generally have no remote player profile or score history to retrieve or delete for you. You may still contact us with any privacy question or rights request.

9. Children

Discore is a general sports utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through a Discore account or backend because neither exists. Parents and guardians should manage app permissions and device backups for younger users.

10. This website

This is a static website. It sets no first-party cookies and includes no custom analytics, advertising pixels, contact forms or externally hosted fonts. If it is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub states that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. If tracking, forms or other services are added later, this policy must be updated before they are enabled.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Discore’s features, dependencies or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top will be revised. Material changes should also be communicated through the app listing or another reasonable channel.
