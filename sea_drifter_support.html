Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. There are no accounts and no cloud saves, so deleting the app deletes your captain, ship and log permanently. Offloading the app (Settings › General › iPhone Storage) keeps the save; deleting it does not.

Co-op will not find my friend's game

Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network. On iPhone and iPad, type the host's address instead of waiting for the scan: the host sees it on their own co-op screen. Allow the Local Network prompt the first time you connect — if you declined it, turn it back on in Settings › Sea Drifter.

What do I need to play?

An iPhone or iPad running iOS 14 or later, held in landscape. The game plays entirely offline; the internet is never required.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. Sea Drifter is a single paid download with no advertising, no in-app purchases and nothing to restore. It collects no personal data — see the privacy policy.

The game crashes, or something looks wrong

Force-quit and reopen first, then restart the device. If it persists, email the details above — crash reports you share through Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics are helpful but never required.
