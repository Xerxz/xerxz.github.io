Privacy Policy

Aniimo Companion · Last updated: October 2, 2026

Aniimo Companion ("the app") is developed by Xerxz. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data stored on your device

Your caught list, saved teams, and egg timers are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. Android's backup system may include it if you have device backup enabled.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.

In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy options.

Learn more in Google's advertising policies .

Purchases

Premium purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

The app only receives confirmation of what you have purchased so it can unlock Premium features.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
