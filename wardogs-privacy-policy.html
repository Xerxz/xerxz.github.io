Wardogs Field Manual Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026. Package: com.xerxz.wardogscompanion. Contact: xerxz5115@gmail.com.

Your local data

Favorites, saved loadouts, cash balance and deployment journal entries are stored locally on your device. The app has no user account, cloud sync, developer analytics service or developer-operated database. It does not access your WARDOGS account. Android backup of app data is disabled. Clear your saved data using More → Clear local data, or uninstall the app.

Advertising and consent

The free app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. These services may process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics and approximate location inferred from IP addresses for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and consent handling. Advertising behavior depends on your consent choices and applicable regional requirements. Where required, a consent form is provided before requesting ads. Where available, use More → Ad privacy choices to change your choices. Premium removes advertising placements.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

Purchases

Google Play processes subscriptions and one-time purchases. The app checks purchase records to determine Premium access and acknowledges completed purchases. We do not receive payment card information. Google Play manages your purchase history, refunds, subscription cancellation and account information. Purchase tokens are used within the Google Play Billing flow; the app does not send them to a developer server.

External references and support

When you open a reference, it loads in your external browser and that website's privacy practices apply. If you email support, your email address and the content you send are used to respond to your request. Do not send account passwords or payment details.

Retention and choices

Local records remain until you delete them or uninstall. We retain support correspondence only as needed to respond and resolve the issue. Google's retention practices are described in its policies. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the contact address above. Cancel subscriptions through Google Play; clearing app data does not cancel a subscription.

Audience

This tactical game reference is intended for a general audience of players and is not directed to children under 13. It does not contain user-to-user messaging or publish user contributions.

Changes

The effective date will change when this policy is updated. Contact the support address for questions.
