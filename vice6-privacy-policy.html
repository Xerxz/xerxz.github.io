Privacy Policy

Vice 6 Companion · Last updated: November 18, 2026

Vice 6 Companion ("the app") is an unofficial, fan-made companion for players following Grand Theft Auto VI, developed by Xerxz. It is available for iPhone and Android. This policy explains what data the app handles.

No tracking of our own

The app does not run its own analytics. The countdown, intel, news and the offline community map are bundled in the app and work without an account. Only the optional Squad feature, described below, uses an account.

Squad (optional account)

Squad lets you add friends and tell them when you start playing. If you sign in to Squad with Apple, Google or email, the app stores the following using Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging):

  • your sign-in identifier and the email address provided by Apple, Google or you;
  • the display name and optional PSN ID you enter;
  • your friend code, friends list, friend requests and blocked players;
  • your check-ins (chosen activity, preset note, start and end time) and preset reactions you send;
  • your time zone, used to avoid notifications at night;
  • a push notification token for your device;
  • reports you file about other players, kept for moderation.

Your friends can see your display name, PSN ID and current check-in. Other signed-in players can see your display name if they have your friend code. Squad does not connect to your PlayStation Network account and never asks for its password. This data is not used for advertising and is not sold.

Notifications are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging and the Apple or Google notification services. You can mute any friend in the app or turn notifications off in your device settings.

Deleting your Squad account

You can delete your Squad account at any time in the app under Squad → profile icon → Delete squad account. This permanently removes your profile, friends, check-ins, requests, reactions, push tokens and sign-in record. You can also request deletion by emailing xerxz5115@gmail.com from the address on your account. Reports about rule-breaking may be kept for up to 90 days to handle abuse, then deleted.

Data stored on your device

The app stores a few settings only on your device, such as whether you have completed the first-launch onboarding. This data is not sent to us and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The free version shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. Depending on your consent, region and device settings, Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID or IDFA), approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. On iPhone, the app asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; the app works the same if you decline. You can revisit your choice under Settings → Ad privacy choices. Premium removes ads.

Learn more in how Google uses data from apps.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with the store which products your account owns so it can unlock Premium. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your App Store or Google Play account.

External links

News, video, community and shopping links open third-party websites that have their own privacy policies. The optional PlayStation 5 shopping link is an Amazon Associates link; Amazon may use cookies and process referral and order information under its own privacy notice. The app does not receive your identity or order details from Amazon.

Children

The app covers a mature-rated game and is not directed to children under 13. Squad requires users to confirm they are 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has created a Squad account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can change ad consent in the app, reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings, restore or manage Premium in your store account, mute friends or turn off notifications, delete your Squad account in the app, and delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the app.
