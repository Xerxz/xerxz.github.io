🗺️ Arc Raiders Interactive Map Guide Quickly navigate and conquer the surface with your in-game map! 1. Finding Materials 💎 Your map is your best tool for material scavenging: Filter/Toggle: Use the map to toggle on icons for material. Zoom In: Zoom in on regions you plan to traverse. Materials are often clustered near points of interest or distinct geographical features. 2. Tracking Quests 🧭 Stay focused on your objectives by actively using the map for quest tracking: Active Quest Icon: The location of your currently Active Quest objective is usually marked by a distinct, icon Select/Toggle Quest: Open your Quests/Objectives Log. Selecting a quest and selecting the Tracking button from the log will automatically update the map to show the target destination. For support join the Discord at: https://discord.gg/fEsVWsg5
