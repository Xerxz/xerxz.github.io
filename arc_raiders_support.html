🗺️ Arc Raiders Interactive Map Guide

Quickly navigate and conquer the surface with your in-game map!

1. Finding Materials 💎

Your map is your best tool for material scavenging:

  • Filter / Toggle: Enable material icons on the map to highlight resource locations.
  • Zoom In: Materials often cluster around points of interest or unique terrain — zoom in to spot them more easily.

2. Tracking Quests 🧭

Stay focused on your objectives by actively using the map for quest tracking:

  • Active Quest Icon: The location of your Active Quest is marked by a unique icon.
  • Select / Track Quest: Open the Quest Log, select a quest, and press Track to automatically highlight the destination.
